Step 4 — Your wealth insights

Methodology & assumptions

FIRE number = annual expenses ÷ withdrawal rate (the 4% rule: 25× annual expenses). Earliest FIRE age comes from a year-by-year projection: your portfolio grows at the real return and you add your monthly savings. Lean targets 70% of current expenses; Fat targets 150%. Coast FIRE asks what you need today so compounding alone reaches the FIRE number by your target age, with no further contributions. Barista FIRE subtracts part-time income from the expenses the portfolio must cover. Income-based FIRE replaces expenses with the monthly passive income you want. Portfolio longevity simulates withdrawing your expenses every year from the portfolio at the same return. Returns are assumed constant and after inflation — real markets are lumpier.
