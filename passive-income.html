Methodology & assumptions

Annual distributions = portfolio × yield. Genuine income = distributions × (1 − return-of-capital %): return of capital is your own money coming back, not profit. Coverage = genuine annual income ÷ annual expenses. The projection grows the portfolio by dividend growth each year, adds reinvested distributions, and subtracts the return-of-capital share that is spent rather than reinvested. Yield, growth and ROC are assumed constant — in reality all three move around.
