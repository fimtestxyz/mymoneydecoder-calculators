Methodology & assumptions

Total net worth = all assets − all liabilities. Investable net worth = cash + stocks, ETFs, bonds and funds — it excludes CPF and property, which are not readily deployable. Liquid assets = cash and deposits only. Debt-to-asset ratio = liabilities ÷ gross assets. Property is valued at your estimate; CPF at your latest statement balance. Figures are a snapshot, not a valuation.
